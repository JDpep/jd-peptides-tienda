# JD Peptides

**Register**: brand-leaning hybrid — design IS part of the trust signal. The store carries scientific products in a legally sensitive category (RUO, research-use-only), so identity and credibility do more selling than UI conventions. Treat catalog/product/checkout as **product register**; treat home, landings, and editorial sections as **brand register**.

## Product purpose

A Mexican direct-to-researcher store for high-purity research peptides. The product is the catalog, the COA documentation, and the chain of trust between synthesis lot → label → buyer.

## Users

Primary: independent researchers, biohackers, scientific hobbyists, small labs in Mexico. They:
- Care about pureza (HPLC %), trazabilidad por lote, and documentation (CoA).
- Distrust generic "peptide stores" that look like supplement shops.
- Cross-reference international vendors (Sigma, Bachem) — JD must look as serious or more serious.
- Read in Spanish but recognize technical English (RUO, COA, HPLC).

Secondary: regulators, payment processors, hosting providers. The site MUST read as legitimate research-grade, not as a thinly-veiled supplement vendor.

## Tone

- Sober and clinical, not corporate-sterile. Warm dark, not cold dashboard.
- Confident, never apologetic, about the RUO disclaimer.
- No hype copy. No "100% NATURAL", no exclamation marks, no growth-hack patterns ("only 2 left!").
- Spanish-first, English allowed for technical terms.

## Anti-references

- Generic Shopify peptide stores (blue + lab coat stock photo + "PURITY GUARANTEED" banner).
- Supplement stores trying to look pharma (white-coat models, gradient hero with pills).
- Crypto/biohacker dark-mode-neon-on-black aesthetic.
- "Lab Brutalism" — green CRT, scrolling DNA helices.

## Strategic principles

1. **The vial is the hero.** Real photos of the actual lot, never stock illustrations. Product photography carries 70% of trust.
2. **Documentation > marketing copy.** A linked CoA card beats three pillar-cards-with-emoji.
3. **Premium gold restraint.** Gold (#c79a3a) is the brand. Use it deliberately as accent, never as fill on large surfaces (it cheapens).
4. **Show the science, not the lifestyle.** No people in lab coats. Molecular structures, lot numbers, certificates.
5. **Mobile-first thumb zones.** Most traffic is mobile WhatsApp links — bottom tab bar, big CTAs, no hover-only affordances.
